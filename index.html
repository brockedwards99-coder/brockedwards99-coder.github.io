What We Do

Two Areas of Focus

Our office is conveniently located in the Metrotown area of Burnaby. We serve the Greater Vancouver region and the rest of British Columbia. We attend courts in New Westminster, Port Coquitlam, Surrey, Richmond, and North Vancouver, and have taken trials throughout British Columbia and Alberta.

⚖️

Criminal Law

Expert criminal defence across British Columbia. We defend clients facing serious charges including impaired driving, hit and run, driving while prohibited, and dangerous driving causing bodily harm or death. Over 40 years of trial experience in BC and Alberta courts.

🚗

Personal Injury Law

If you've been injured due to negligence, we fight to secure the compensation you deserve. We specialize in motor vehicle accident claims and handle cases throughout the Greater Vancouver region and beyond. Compassionate, skilled representation when you need it most.

Biographies

Brock A. Edwards, LL.B.

Criminal & Personal Injury · Motor Vehicle Accidents

Brock Edwards practices criminal law and personal injury, with a focus on motor vehicle accidents. He articled in Calgary and has worked on a wide range of matters including jury trials, dangerous driving causing death, and impaired driving causing bodily harm.

He was called to the Alberta Bar in 2001 and admitted to the British Columbia Bar in 2004, accumulating over 15 years of law office experience.
